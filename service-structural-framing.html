Scope

What’s Included

The scope is confirmed with you before work starts, so you know exactly what will be measured and priced.

01

Structural steel

Beams, columns, bracing and purlins measured by section size and length, then converted to tonnes; plates and connections from the details.

02

Concrete frames

Columns, beams, slabs and walls in m³ by element and level, with formwork in m² and reinforcement in tonnes.

03

Timber & light-gauge

Studs, plates, noggings, lintels, joists, bearers and rafters in lineal metres by size and grade; trusses and connectors counted.

04

Foundations

Pad and strip footings, pile caps and ground beams by element; piles by number and length.

05

By member type

Tonnage, lengths and counts grouped by member type, section size and level.

06

Schedules

Structural schedules and summaries for pricing.
