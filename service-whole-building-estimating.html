Scope

What’s Included

The scope is confirmed with you before work starts, so you know exactly what will be measured and priced.

01

Substructure

Excavation, footings, ground slabs, basements and below-ground drainage.

02

Frame & upper floors

Concrete, steel or timber frames, suspended slabs, stairs and reinforcement.

03

Roof

Roof structure, coverings, flashings, insulation and roof drainage.

04

External envelope

External walls, cladding, windows, external doors and glazing.

05

Internal walls & doors

Partitions, linings, internal doors and ironmongery.

06

Finishes

Wall, floor and ceiling finishes, tiling and painting.

07

Fittings & joinery

Kitchens, joinery, sanitary fittings and fixed equipment.

08

Building services

Electrical, plumbing and mechanical services, plus fire services and lifts where shown.

09

External works & preliminaries

Paving, landscaping and site drainage, with preliminaries, contingencies and margins as separate items.

Deliverables

What You Receive

  • 01

    Complete Bill of Quantities in Excel, covering every trade and formula-linked to the summary

  • 02

    Estimate summary by trade or by element, with cost per m² of gross floor area

  • 03

    Material, labour and plant breakdowns (when pricing is requested)

  • 04

    Marked-up drawings showing what was measured

  • 05

    Assumptions, exclusions, provisional sums and clarifications

Ideal for

Who It’s For

  • Design and build contractors
  • Developers and property owners
  • Quantity surveyors needing extra capacity
