Scope

What’s Included

The scope is confirmed with you before work starts, so you know exactly what will be measured and priced.

01

Document review

Drawings, specifications, pricing schedules and tender conditions reviewed at the start, with missing information and scope gaps flagged.

02

Quantities & BOQs

Measured to the method the tender calls for — ASMM, NRM2, CSI MasterFormat, the CIQS method or NZS 4202 — or set out in the bill issued with the tender.

03

Trade packages

Quantities split by trade package so subcontractor quotes can be requested and compared like for like.

04

Pricing support

Priced with your rates and supplier quotes, with material, labour and plant shown separately when requested.

05

Clarifications

Assumptions, exclusions and qualifications listed, plus queries to raise where the documents are unclear.

06

Addenda

Each addendum and drawing revision logged, affected quantities updated and changes highlighted against the previous issue.

    Marked-up drawings showing what was measured
