Scope

What’s Included

The scope is confirmed with you before work starts, so you know exactly what will be measured and priced.

01

Roof areas

Each plane measured on the slope, so areas in m² are pitch-adjusted rather than plan areas.

02

Coverings

Sheets by profile and length, tiles from the coverage rate per m², or membrane in m² with laps, plus fixings where specified.

03

Flashings & trims

Ridges, hips, valleys, barges, apron and wall flashings in lineal metres, with hips and valleys at true length.

04

Insulation

Insulation, sarking and underlay in m², allowing for laps.

05

Roof drainage

Gutters and downpipes in lineal metres; sumps, outlets and rainheads counted.

06

Pricing

Labour and material pricing where requested.

Deliverables

What You Receive

  • 01

    Roofing quantity schedule — areas in m², flashings and drainage in lineal metres

  • 02

    Material list

  • 03

    Cost estimate (if requested)

  • 04

    Marked-up roof plans

  • 05

    Assumptions and exclusions, including any assumed roof pitch
