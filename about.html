Who we are

Estimators Who Understand How Projects Are Built

Every quote and tender — whether to bid, how to price, where the margin sits — depends on reliable numbers. We help contractors and subcontractors get those numbers right.

Our work starts with the trades: roofing, cladding, structural, electrical and plumbing estimates, through to complete whole building estimates. We also provide quantity takeoffs, Bills of Quantities, tender support and dedicated estimator arrangements, as well as civil and earthworks estimates. We work from your drawings and specifications, mark up exactly what we measured and document our assumptions so every figure can be understood and checked.

Our company

Engineering-Led Estimating for Clients Worldwide

Vision Estimators is the construction estimating team of Remote Teams IT Solutions. For more than five years Remote Teams has provided engineering design services, estimating and dedicated remote staff to businesses around the world.

Every estimator on our team is qualified to engineering degree level or equivalent. That grounding — together with a structured process and open, transparent communication — goes into every trade estimate, takeoff and BOQ we prepare for contractors, subcontractors and builders in Australia, the USA, the UK, Canada and New Zealand.

We work the way a good in-house estimator would: we read the whole drawing set, measure methodically, state our assumptions and deliver in the format your team already uses.

What drives us

Mission, Approach, Expertise & Commitment

01

Our Mission

To give contractors and subcontractors clear, reliable estimates so they can price work with confidence and tender for the right jobs.

02

Our Approach

We start with the drawings and scope, measure and structure quantities to your market’s standard — ASMM, NRM2, CIQS, NZS 4202 or CSI MasterFormat — and deliver information that is easy to check and use.

03

Our Expertise

Roofing, cladding, structural, electrical, plumbing and whole building estimates, plus takeoffs, BOQs, tender support and dedicated estimators — for residential, commercial and industrial work, with civil and earthworks estimating too.

04

Our Commitment

Clear communication, honest assumptions and deliverables that stand up to review — on every project, large or small.
