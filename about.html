About Vision Estimators

international Estimating Built on Trust & Precision

We’re a dedicated team of senior estimators and quantity surveyors helping builders worldwide win tenders and deliver projects on budget — across the globe.

Our Story

Driven by International Industry Insight

Vision Estimators is a specialised construction estimation division of Remote Teams Pty Ltd, dedicated exclusively to providing accurate and reliable estimating solutions for builders and construction companies worldwide. We deliver quantity take-offs, cost estimations, BOQ preparation, and tender support tailored to international construction standards, the international building codes and each client’s local standards (AS, NRM, RICS, RSMeans).

Estimating built exclusively for builders worldwide

As a dedicated estimation division within Remote Teams Pty Ltd, we focus exclusively on international construction estimating — preparing every deliverable to international construction standards and each region’s local building regulations. Senior estimators handle each project, applying deep insight into how work is priced, tendered, and won across NSW, VIC, QLD, WA, SA, TAS, ACT, and NT.

What we deliver to builders worldwide

  • Quantity take-offs from drawings and specifications to international standards
  • Cost estimations using current international market rates by state and region
  • BOQ preparation aligned with international tender and contract requirements
  • Tender support compliant with international procurement processes
