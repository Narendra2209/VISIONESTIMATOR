The service

What Is a Dedicated Estimator?

A dedicated estimator is an estimating professional who works on your projects on an ongoing basis. Rather than sending out individual jobs and briefing someone new each time, you work with the same person — someone who gets to know your drawings, your trades, your templates and how you like estimates presented.

They handle the measuring, quantifying and organising work that takes up so much of an estimating team’s time, so your in-house people can focus on pricing decisions, client relationships and winning work.

Your estimator works remotely as a dedicated member of your team, using the communication and project tools agreed with you. Vision Estimators is the construction estimating team of Remote Teams IT Solutions, which has served clients worldwide for more than five years.
