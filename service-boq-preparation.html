Scope

What’s Included

The scope is confirmed with you before work starts, so you know exactly what will be measured and priced.

01

Item descriptions

Clear, consistent descriptions for each measurable item, with references to the specification where one is provided.

02

Logical structure

Grouped by trade, work section or element.

03

Measured quantities

Net quantities measured from the drawings in m, m², m³, t or nr (or imperial units), each traceable to the takeoff.

04

Provisional items

Preliminaries, provisional sums and allowances where required.

05

Pricing columns

Rate and amount columns with formula-linked totals that roll up to the collection and summary pages.

06

Method or template

Measured to ASMM, NRM2, CSI MasterFormat, the CIQS method or NZS 4202 — or set out in your own BOQ template.

Deliverables

What You Receive

  • 01

  • 02

    Summary and collection pages

  • 03

    Supporting takeoff and marked-up PDF drawings showing what was measured

  • 04

    Assumptions, exclusions and clarifications

Common Questions

A BOQ lists the measured items of work in a project with descriptions, units and quantities so that each item can be priced consistently.

Yes. We can prepare the BOQ in your preferred structure and format, or propose a structure suited to the project and the measurement method used in your market. Units (metric or imperial) and currency are agreed at the start.

It can be issued unpriced for tendering, or priced as part of a cost estimate — whichever you need. If priced, we use your own rates or market-based rates agreed with you before pricing.

Standard methods such as ASMM and NRM2 measure work net as fixed in place, so waste is normally allowed for in the rates rather than the quantities. If your template asks for allowances in the quantities, the percentages used are listed in the assumptions.
