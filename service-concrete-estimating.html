Concrete EstimatingServices

Takeoffs and estimates for concrete, formwork and reinforcement contractors — volumes, bar and mesh quantities, formwork areas and related items measured from your drawings, organised by element and pour for pricing and ordering.

Scope

What’s Included

The scope is confirmed with you before work starts, so you know exactly what will be measured and priced.

01

Concrete volumes

Measured in m³ by element, strength grade and level or pour.

02

Reinforcement

Bar tonnage by diameter from bar schedules, or a stated kg/m³ allowance per element where none exist; mesh in m² by type, with laps as agreed.

03

Formwork

Contact area in m² by face — slab soffits and edges, beam sides, column and wall faces — split by finish.

04

Structural elements

Footings, slabs, walls, columns and beams.

05

Civil concrete

Kerbs and channels in lineal metres, pavements in m², headwalls by number and volume.

06

Accessories

Joints and waterstops in lineal metres, membranes in m² and cast-in items counted, where shown.

Deliverables

What You Receive

  • 01

    Concrete schedule in m³ by element, grade and level

  • 02

    Reinforcement summary in tonnes by bar diameter, with mesh in m²

  • 03

    Formwork schedule in m² by element and finish

  • 04

    Pour-by-pour breakdown if required

  • 05

    Marked-up drawings and a list of assumptions, including any reinforcement allowances
