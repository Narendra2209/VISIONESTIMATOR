Our process

A Clear ProcessFrom Drawings to Delivery

Eight structured steps take your trade package or whole building from the first set of drawings to a checked estimate, ready for your quote or tender — with clear communication at every stage.

Send Project Information See the Steps

Step by step

How Every Estimate Is Prepared

  1. STEP 01

    Send Project Information

    Share drawings (PDF, DWG or DXF), specifications, addenda, the trades you are pricing and your deadline through the contact form or by email. Larger drawing sets can be sent as a download link.

  2. STEP 02

    Scope Review

    We review the documents, confirm what is included, agree the measurement method, units and deliverables, and confirm your delivery date before work begins. Standard estimates are typically delivered within 48 hours.

  3. STEP 03

    Drawing & Specification Analysis

    Drawings, specifications and addenda are cross-checked, and any conflicts, missing sheets or unclear details are raised with you as queries before measuring starts.

  4. STEP 04

    Quantity Takeoff

    Elements are measured to the agreed method — such as ASMM, NRM2, CSI MasterFormat, the CIQS method or NZS 4202, or your own template — and organised by trade, with marked-up drawings showing what was measured.

  5. STEP 05

    Cost & Labour Analysis

    When pricing is requested, material, labour and plant costs are built up using agreed rates, or your own rates on request.

  6. STEP 06

    BOQ / Estimate Preparation

    Quantities and costs are compiled into a formula-linked Excel BOQ and estimate, with assumptions, exclusions and clarifications listed.

  7. STEP 07

    Quality Review

    Every estimate is checked for completeness, consistency and arithmetic before it is delivered.

  8. STEP 08

    Final Delivery

    You receive the estimate, BOQ and marked-up drawings on the confirmed date — and we stay available for questions.
