What You Receive

Deliverables are organised by trade so your team can review, price and quote straight away — in the formats you already work with.

01

Quantity takeoffs

Trade-by-trade quantities in formula-linked Excel, with marked-up PDF drawings showing what was measured.

02

Bills of Quantities

Itemised BOQs to ASMM, NRM2, CSI MasterFormat, the CIQS method, NZS 4202 or your own template.

03

Cost estimates

Material, labour and plant breakdowns when pricing is requested.

04

Clarifications

Assumptions, exclusions and questions stated clearly.

What we work from

Send What You Have

Most trade estimates start with PDF drawings and a short note of the trades you are pricing. If anything is missing, we will tell you before work begins. Standard estimates are typically delivered within 48 hours; for larger projects we confirm the delivery date after reviewing the scope.

Send Your Drawings
  • 01

    Drawings

    Architectural, structural and services drawings (electrical, plumbing and mechanical) — PDF sets or DWG/DXF CAD files.

  • 02

    Specifications & schedules

    Roofing and cladding systems, finishes, fixture and light fitting schedules — anything that defines quality and scope.

  • 03

    Scope of work

    Which trades or packages you are pricing — or the whole building — and to what level of detail.

  • 04

    Tender documents

    Tender or quote conditions, addenda, pricing schedules and submission requirements.

  • 05

    Your preferences

    Measurement method, units, currency, rates, templates and the deadline you are working to.

  • 06

    Survey & levels

    For civil and earthworks jobs only: survey data, LandXML surfaces, design levels, sections and geotechnical reports.
