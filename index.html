The estimating journey

From drawings to a tender-ready estimate

  1. Architectural, structural and services drawings are reviewed together with the specification and scope.
  2. Plans, sections and elevations are read as one building — levels, elements and interfaces.
  3. Every element is measured. Lengths, areas and volumes are taken off and checked against dimensions.
  4. Quantities are organised by trade — excavation, concrete, reinforcement, formwork, masonry and glazing.
  5. Measured quantities become a structured BOQ with clear descriptions and units, ready for pricing.
  6. Material, labour and plant rates are applied to each item to build a clear cost picture.
  7. Summary, BOQ, takeoff records and clarifications — organised into a tender-ready package.

Built for Contractors & Subcontractors

Whether you are pricing one trade package or the whole job, we measure your scope from the drawings and set out quantities in the units you work in — ready for your rates, your template and your tender.

    Roofing contractors

    Pitch-adjusted roof areas in m², with ridges, valleys, flashings and gutters in lm.

  • 04

    Electrical contractors

    Fitting and device counts, with cabling, conduit and cable tray in lm.

  • 05

    Plumbing contractors

    Pipework in lm by service, with fixture, valve and fitting counts.

    Builders & head contractors
