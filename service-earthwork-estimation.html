Scope

What’s Included

The scope is confirmed with you before work starts, including the calculation method, strip depths and factors, so you know exactly what will be measured and priced.

01

Cut & fill volumes

Volumes between the existing surface and design subgrade (finished levels less pavement, slab or topsoil depths), split by area, lot or stage.

02

Excavation

Bulk excavation, including basements, and detailed excavation for footings and pits, with batters and working space where they add volume.

03

Topsoil

Strip depth over the site area, stockpile volumes and respread to landscaped areas.

04

Trenching

Service trenches by depth band, with bedding, pipe surround and backfill measured from the trench details.

05

Import & export

Surplus spoil to export and fill to import, after reuse of suitable site-won material.

06

Factors

Bulking and compaction factors, taken from your geotechnical report or instructions and stated as assumptions.

Method

How Volumes Are Calculated

The method depends on the information supplied. It is agreed at scope review and stated in the deliverable, so every volume can be traced back to the surfaces and levels used.

01

Surface to surface

The existing and design surfaces are built as triangulated models from LandXML, CAD or surveyed levels and compared across the site. Best suited to bulk earthworks, building pads and subdivisions.

02

Cross-sections

Cut and fill areas are measured on sections at set chainages and averaged between them (average end area). Suited to roads, channels, embankments and other linear works.

03

Grid levels

Existing and design levels are compared at each point of a regular grid, and the cut or fill depth is averaged over each cell. Used where only spot levels or a level survey are available.
