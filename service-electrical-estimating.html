Scope

What’s Included

The scope is confirmed with you before work starts, so you know exactly what will be measured and priced.

01

Lighting

Light fittings counted by type from the lighting layouts and schedules, including emergency and exit lighting.

02

Power

Power outlets, switches, isolators and equipment connections counted by type.

03

Cabling

Sub-mains and final-circuit cabling in lineal metres by type and size, with routes stated where layouts are schematic.

04

Containment

Cable tray, ladder, conduit and trunking in lineal metres by type and size, with bends and fittings.

05

Switchboards & distribution

Main switchboards, distribution boards and metering scheduled from the single-line diagrams.

06

Communications & security

Data outlets, CCTV, access control and fire detection devices counted where shown on the drawings.

Deliverables

What You Receive

  • 01

    Electrical quantity schedule — fittings and devices counted, cabling and containment in lineal metres

  • 02

    Summaries by system, level or distribution board

  • 03

    Cost estimate with material and labour breakdown (when pricing is requested)

  • 04

    Marked-up electrical drawings showing what was measured

  • 05

    Assumptions for cable routes and lengths where layouts are schematic
