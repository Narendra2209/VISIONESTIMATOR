Privacy Policy

How we collect, use, store, and protect the information you share with Vision Estimators.

Last updated: 10 May 2026

1. Introduction

Vision Estimators (“we”, “us”, “our”) provides construction cost estimating, quantity surveying, and tender support services to builders and contractors worldwide. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle personal and project information you provide when you contact us, request a quote, or engage our services. It is intended to comply with the international Privacy Principles (APPs) under the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth).

By using our website or services, you consent to the practices described in this policy.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information You Provide

  • Contact details — name, company, email address, and phone number submitted via our contact form or email.
  • Project information — drawings, specifications, BIM files, scope notes, and any documents you share to obtain an estimate.
  • Correspondence — any messages, briefs, or feedback you send us.

2.2 Information Collected Automatically

  • Basic technical data such as browser type, device, IP address, and pages visited (used solely to operate and improve the site).
  • Anonymous analytics data, where enabled, to understand site traffic patterns.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide to:

  • Prepare cost estimates, quantity takeoffs, and tender documents you have requested;
  • Respond to enquiries and provide quotes;
  • Issue invoices and process payments for engaged work;
  • Send service-related communications (revisions, clarifications, delivery notices);
  • Improve our website, services, and client experience.

We do not sell or rent your personal data to third parties under any circumstances.

4. Sharing & Disclosure

Your information may be shared only with:

  • Internal estimators and project staff directly working on your engagement;
  • Trusted service providers (e.g. cloud storage, email delivery) bound by confidentiality obligations;
  • Legal authorities, where disclosure is required by law or to protect our rights.

All project files and drawings are treated as confidential and are not shared outside your engagement.

5. Data Storage & Security

Project files and personal data are stored on access-controlled cloud infrastructure. We apply reasonable technical and organisational safeguards (encryption in transit, access restrictions, regular access reviews) to protect against unauthorised access, alteration, or loss.

While no system can guarantee absolute security, we take the protection of your data seriously and act promptly to address any incident.

6. Data Retention

We retain project files and related correspondence for a reasonable period after delivery, typically up to 24 months, to support revisions, audit, and follow-on work. After this period, files are securely deleted unless you request earlier removal or longer retention.

7. Your Rights

Subject to applicable law, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you;
  • Request correction of inaccurate information;
  • Request deletion of your information;
  • Withdraw consent for ongoing communications;
  • Lodge a complaint with the relevant data protection authority.

To exercise any of these rights, email info@visionestimators.com.

8. Cookies

Our website uses minimal cookies, limited to those required for basic functionality and anonymous analytics. You can disable cookies in your browser settings; the site will continue to function normally.

9. Data Location

Vision Estimators serves clients exclusively worldwide. Your information is stored on access-controlled cloud infrastructure and handled in accordance with the international Privacy Principles. Where any service provider operates from outside India, we apply appropriate safeguards consistent with international data-protection requirements.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision. Material changes will be highlighted on our website.

11. Contact Us

For privacy-related questions or requests, contact us at:

  • Email: info@visionestimators.com
  • Web: www.visionestimators.com
  • Coverage: Worldwide — Australia, USA, UK, Canada, New Zealand & more
