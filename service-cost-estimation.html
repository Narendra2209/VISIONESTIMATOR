Scope

What’s Included

The scope is confirmed with you before work starts, so you know exactly what will be measured and priced.

01

Rate build-ups

Each rate split into material, labour and plant, with waste and productivity assumptions shown so you can check them or apply your own.

02

Any design stage

Estimates at concept, design development or tender stage.

03

Clear summaries

Costs summarised by trade, element or stage, or grouped by the work sections of your market’s method — ASMM, NRM2, CSI MasterFormat, CIQS or NZS 4202.

04

Preliminaries

Site establishment, supervision, temporary works and other preliminaries or general conditions priced as separate items.

Agreed pricing basis

Your rates, supplier quotes or market-based rates agreed before pricing, in the currency agreed at the start.

06

Option comparisons

Side-by-side costs for design or specification options.
